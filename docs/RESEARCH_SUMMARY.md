# HireNudge research summary

Research cut-off: **6 August 2026**  
Scope: repository and operating briefs; 144 supplied product screenshots; 296 supplied competitor ad assets with a disclosed stratified sample; HireNudge public pages; competitor primary sources; job/API, OAuth, contact-data, regulatory, accessibility and creative-tool documentation.  
Decision status: **all recommendations are To Be Discussed unless subsequently approved by the founder.** No dashboard or application redesign was built.

## Executive decision

HireNudge should not compete on the number of AI job-search features or applications sent. Its most defensible near-term hypothesis is a focused, India-first system that helps an active job switcher choose a worthwhile role, build one truthful job-specific application packet, keep the next step clear and learn which high-quality applications produce interviews.

Before redesign or acquisition scale, the company needs three things: trust remediation, reliable activation/outcome instrumentation and a paid first-100 cohort. Job-data quality and outreach permissions are product foundations, not backend details.

## 15 strongest findings

1. HireNudge visibly covers much of the journey, but the UI is module/card-led rather than job- and outcome-led (`SRC-SS-HN`).
2. No analytics export, cohort retention, customer interviews, support corpus, cancellation reasons, unit economics or verified outcome dataset was supplied.
3. Repository initiatives are `Proposed`, with `approvedCount: 0`; team discussion must not be interpreted as approval or execution.
4. Numeric match percentages are visually prominent, but no scientifically valid hiring-probability model or explanation benchmark was supplied.
5. Public pricing markets bulk/autopilot behavior while the [privacy policy](https://hirenudge.ai/privacy-policy/) stresses review and the extension's no-auto-submit behavior; claims and controls conflict.
6. The homepage's `10,000+`, outcome testimonials and employer logos are public claims for which substantiation and permissions were not supplied ([homepage](https://hirenudge.ai/)).
7. The public portfolio reference exposes identity-document numbers. This is an immediate privacy remediation, not a future design backlog item.
8. Gmail is requested as onboarding completion before an outreach use case. Google permits narrower send-only access; broader scopes add verification/security burden ([Gmail scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes)).
9. A contact provider's API or privacy policy does not give HireNudge a lawful basis to enrich/send; country, purpose, notice, objection and suppression obligations remain.
10. Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever expose per-company public posting surfaces, not universal licensed feeds. Job rights, freshness and deletion need source-specific design.
11. Competitors converge on the same broad suite. Their strongest pattern is workflow connection—job, materials, tracker and stage—not feature count.
12. The supplied ads consistently use creator-native hooks, clear pain and product artifacts; however, no performance data supports calling any creative a winner.
13. NxtJob.ai's India/Hinglish localization is strategically relevant, while its dramatic interview/hidden-job/agent claims illustrate what HireNudge should not repeat without proof.
14. Voice interview practice is technically feasible, but content/rubric feedback is more defensible than confidence, emotion, accent or employability scoring.
15. Targeting India domestic, India-to-global, foreign-to-foreign and all remote/sponsorship segments simultaneously would dilute data, product and distribution learning.

## 15 strongest recommendations

1. Remove public identity-document numbers immediately and scan every public portfolio/output for sensitive identifiers.
2. Create a claims register and remove/qualify every unsupported user, logo, testimonial, outcome, `ATS`, bulk and autopilot claim.
3. Define activation as one live job + reviewed alignment + truthful application packet + next action—not profile completion or Gmail connection.
4. Instrument the full job-centered funnel and outcome denominator before paid scale.
5. Adopt a canonical candidate evidence profile with source, confirmation and sensitivity flags.
6. Make an application packet the core product object linking job, documents, outreach, tracker, interview and outcome.
7. Replace score-first matching with requirements, candidate evidence, gaps, unknowns and correction controls; call it profile-to-role alignment.
8. Implement a job-provenance/freshness/expiry/dedupe layer and benchmark it on a manually reviewed sample.
9. Use curated ATS boards and licensed partnerships; do not make restricted-platform scraping the core supply.
10. Start Gmail with `gmail.send`, connect at the moment of sending, require review for every message and revoke/delete tokens on disconnect.
11. Pilot only one contact provider and one outreach use case after contract, privacy and legal review; maintain suppression and low limits.
12. Build transcript-first, job/round-specific interview practice; add opt-in voice only after human-calibrated evaluation and retention controls.
13. Start with India-based employed switchers, 1–5 years, 30–60-day intent; choose one or two role families from internal evidence.
14. Acquire 100 paying users in four cohorts of 25 and change the product between cohorts based on behavior and outcome data.
15. Prototype the job-centered information architecture and accessible design system; defer mascot/maze/full redesign until message and activation evidence exists.

## Most important product gaps

- Canonical profile/evidence provenance.
- Base-versus-job-specific artifact versioning.
- Application packet and outcome-linked tracker.
- Explainable, correctable alignment.
- Job source/freshness/expiry/deduplication.
- Search-status state: active, interviewing, paused, offer, ended.
- In-product export, deletion and OAuth revocation.
- Event taxonomy, cohorts and AI-quality evaluation sets.
- Claims, model/provider and subprocessor governance.

## Most important UX gaps

- No single dominant next step on the visible dashboard.
- Module-first navigation makes the user assemble the journey.
- Scores and credits carry more weight than evidence and value.
- Gmail permission appears too early.
- Empty tracker is not seeded by onboarding's target job.
- Version/history/undo and AI-edit provenance are not evident.
- Job freshness/provenance needs a comprehensible visible state.
- Accessibility, reduced-motion and non-voice equivalence have no supplied QA evidence.

## Strongest new features

1. Candidate evidence vault.
2. Job-centered application packet.
3. Requirement evidence map.
4. Job provenance card.
5. Truth-preserving achievement coach.
6. Packet-level outcome learning.
7. India hiring-context fields.
8. Transcript-first interview coach.
9. Privacy-safe portfolio publisher for selected role families.

- Founder-led `Qualified Application Clinic` and anonymized teardowns.
- `Fresh or Dead Job?` source/freshness educational utility.
- Notice-period/search-plan calculator.
- Role-family clinics with career coaches/creators.
- Original India job-switch content in English/Hinglish.

- Referral request only after a verified packet/practice value event.
- University/placement-cell pilot only after D2C activation is demonstrated.

## Do not pursue yet

- Fully autonomous or high-volume applications/outreach.
- Inbox-reading personalization for a send-only problem.
- Global multi-corridor launch or foreign-to-foreign expansion.
- Emotion, personality, honesty, accent or employability scoring.
- Employer-side screening/ranking.
- Large template marketplace or broad portfolio expansion.
- Programmatic SEO at scale without original decision utility.
- Full mascot/3D/maze/pinball redesign.
- Paid acquisition scale before activation, retention, claim and margin gates.

## Legal or security blockers

- Public sensitive-identifier exposure.
- Missing claim substantiation/logo/testimonial permissions.
- Job-source commercial rights, scraping restrictions and takedown rules.
- Gmail scope/verification, token security and revocation evidence.
- Contact-data lawful basis, notices, objections, suppression and country rules.
- CAN-SPAM, GDPR/ePrivacy, UK PECR and DPDP use-case analysis.
- TerraTern cross-use consent and controller-role decision.
- Audio/transcript consent, retention, accessibility and prohibited scoring.
- Missing security control evidence: RBAC, audit logs, incident response and extension review.

## Initial ICP recommendation

Start with English-speaking, India-based employed professionals with approximately 1–5 years of experience who plan to switch within 30–60 days. Pick one or two role families only after reviewing current users, job coverage and distribution access. Treat India-to-one-country sponsorship as a separate later pilot; do not bundle it into the initial ICP.

## First-100-customer approach

Run four paid cohorts of 25 through a bounded Application Quality Sprint: profile/evidence, two live roles, two reviewed packets, tracker/follow-up and relevant interview practice. Recruit through founder/alumni reach, role communities, two disclosed coach/creator partners, activated-user referrals and only a small retargeting cell. Observe Day 1 activation and interview at Days 0, 7 and 21/30. Calibrate thresholds with the first 10 users, then gate each subsequent cohort on event reliability, repeated real use, trust controls and willingness to pay.
