# Social and advertisement strategy

## Decision

Build an organic learning engine now; do not scale paid acquisition until activation and D7 repeat use are instrumented. The 296 supplied creatives inform formats, not proven performance.

## Archive findings

The full method and brand table are in `02_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`. Strong transferable signals are creator-native delivery, a clear first-frame problem, product proof, captions and one CTA. The riskiest signals are unsupported percentages, fake-news advertorial framing, artificial scarcity and promises of interview volume or hidden jobs.

## Original content pillars

1. **Application quality:** anonymized job/resume breakdowns and truthful rewrites.
2. **Job-search systems:** source freshness, tracker decisions, follow-up logic.
3. **India realities:** notice period, CTC, campus-to-career and local hiring context.
4. **Mobility truth:** sponsorship evidence, eligibility questions and scam avoidance.
5. **Interview practice:** answer structure, examples and round-specific preparation.
6. **Build in public:** founder decisions, experiments and what HireNudge refuses to automate.

## Paid-ad readiness gates

Paid scale begins only when:

- activation definition and attribution are stable;
- at least two cohorts show acceptable D7 repeat use;
- the landing claim has substantiation;
- onboarding completes without Gmail requirement;
- product events connect creative → signup → activation → paid outcome;
- CAC ceiling derives from contribution margin, not competitor spend.

## Production workflow

Research brief → claim check → script/storyboard → original asset → accessibility captions/alt text → legal/brand review where needed → tracked URL → post → 7/14/30-day learning note. AI may generate variants, but a human approves facts, likenesses, testimonials, cultural context and final edit.

Use Remotion/HyperFrames for repeatable product demonstrations, HeyGen only with disclosed and consented likeness/voice, and image/video generation for original concepts—not simulated customers.

## Measurement

Organic: qualified profile visits, saves, meaningful comments, tool starts, activated signups.  
Paid: thumb-stop/video completion as diagnostics, then landing conversion, activation, D7 repeat use, paid conversion and contribution-margin CAC.  
Do not optimize to followers, impressions or CTR alone.
