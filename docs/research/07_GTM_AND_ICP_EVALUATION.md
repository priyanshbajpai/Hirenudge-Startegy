# GTM and ICP evaluation

## Initial ICP recommendation

Provisional wedge: **English-speaking, India-based employed professionals with roughly 1–5 years of experience who intend to switch private-sector roles in the next 30–60 days.** Select one or two role families only after inspecting current users, job coverage and team access. This remains a hypothesis requiring founder confirmation and paid behavior.

Why this wedge:

- urgency creates repeated workflow need;
- existing experience supplies evidence to improve rather than invent;
- India-specific constraints can differentiate from global generic tools;
- the team can test through direct communities, alumni, creators and workshops;
- it avoids requiring international job-data/immigration breadth on day one.

## Corridor evaluation

| Corridor | User need | Product/data burden | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| India → India | Relevant jobs, CTC, notice period, location, role fit, follow-up | Medium | Job freshness and outcome claims | **Start here** with chosen role families |
| India → foreign country | Sponsorship, authorization, country CV, relocation and verified roles | High | Immigration accuracy, stale sponsor evidence, fraud | One destination/role pilot after domestic core |
| Foreign → foreign | Multiple legal regimes, languages and market conventions | Very high | No clear distribution or proprietary advantage | P3; do not pursue yet |
| Remote across corridors | Employer location, payroll/EOR, timezone, work authorization, contractor status | High | `Remote` does not equal globally employable | Treat as constraint within a chosen corridor, not separate broad ICP |
| Sponsorship-dependent | Evidence of sponsor history and current role eligibility | High | False assurance can cause material harm | Show source/uncertainty; never guarantee sponsorship |

## Positioning test

Control: broad all-in-one AI job-search suite.  
Variant A: higher-quality applications for Indian job switchers.  
Variant B: evidence-backed job search from role fit to interview.  
Variant C: India-to-[country] sponsorship-aware job search (only in pilot).

Measure qualified cohort signup, first packet completion, D7 return and willingness to pay—not message preference alone.

## Channel sequence

1. Founder/alumni/operator network and direct research recruitment.
2. Role-family communities and small paid clinics.
3. Career coaches and creators with transparent referral attribution.
4. University/placement-cell research, later than D2C proof.
5. SEO/free tools after conversion path works.
6. Paid social after activation and margin gates.

## Partnerships

- Career coaches: co-run application clinics; do not imply placement guarantees.
- Creators: original educational briefs, disclosed commercial relationship, outcome-safe copy.
- Universities: cohort readiness pilot only after consumer activation; explicit student data terms.
- TerraTern: distribution or mobility expertise only with purpose-specific opt-in, separate access and clear controller roles.

## GTM non-goals

- all job seekers;
- unemployed, student, senior executive and international cohorts simultaneously;
- high-volume auto-apply as acquisition hook;
- employer-logo borrowing without permission;
- paid-media scale on top of unverified activation;
- mixing TerraTern leads into HireNudge campaigns without explicit consent.
