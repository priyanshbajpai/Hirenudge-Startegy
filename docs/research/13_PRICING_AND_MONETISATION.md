# Pricing and monetisation

## Current public offer

As observed on [HireNudge pricing](https://hirenudge.ai/pricing/):

| Plan | Displayed term | Displayed price | Credits | Outreach |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Free | Lifetime | `$0` | 30 | 10 |
| Pro | 30 days | `$19 + 18% GST` (`$22.42`) | 120 | 100 |
| Career Boost | 90 days | `$45 + 18% GST` (`$53.10`) | 500 | 300 |

This is public packaging, not validated willingness to pay. The page's `unlimited`/credit wording and bulk/autopilot language should be reconciled with actual entitlements and safety controls.

## Market patterns

Competitors commonly keep tracking/base-resume utility free and charge for AI depth, job-specific materials, advanced analysis or short search periods. Weekly plans recognize episodic urgency but can create renewal/refund friction. Multi-month plans align with job-search duration. Current examples are recorded in `02_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`; they are not India price anchors.

## Recommended tests

### Packaging hypothesis

- **Free foundation:** base profile, tracker, limited job saves, source/freshness view, one alignment explanation and export.
- **Application Quality Sprint:** 2 or 4 weeks; job-specific packets, advanced explanations, reminders and limited interview practice.
- **Active Search:** monthly or 8 weeks; higher packet/practice/outreach limits.
- **Human expert add-on:** resume/interview clinic with explicit deliverable and service terms.

Show INR and tax-inclusive totals for India; state renewal, expiry, refund and credit rollover before purchase. Do not call an action unlimited if credits constrain it.

### Experiment dimensions

| Dimension | Cells | Primary measure | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 2-week, 4-week, monthly/8-week | Paid activation and contribution margin | Refund, confusion, support |
| Meter | Packet/session allowance vs transparent credits | Comprehension and usage | Surprise depletion |
| Price | 2–3 INR levels based on interviews/COGS | Conversion × margin | Outcome quality |
| Trial | Free value event vs time trial | Activated paid conversion | Low-intent abuse |
| Human add-on | None vs clinic | Attach rate and satisfaction | Capacity and service quality |

Do not pick numeric INR prices before the first 10–15 pricing interviews and provider/unit-cost model. The first-100 cohort can test them sequentially.

## Credit design

- Meter costly outputs users understand: completed packet analysis, realtime interview minute, verified-contact reveal.
- Do not charge for corrections, safety warnings, failed jobs or regenerating an error.
- Preview the cost and resulting balance before action.
- Publish whether credits expire/roll over and the refund effect.
- Keep outreach limits independent from Gmail/platform reputation caps.

## Unit economics required

Per plan and feature: model/audio tokens, job-data calls, enrichment, email verification, rendering/storage, payment fees, support/human time, taxes, refunds, failed payments and partner commissions. Report contribution margin by activated user and completed search episode, not gross subscription revenue alone.

## B2B and institutional

University/placement-cell and coach bundles are plausible later. They require student consent, role separation, aggregate reporting and no placement guarantees. Do not customize the product for institutional buyers until consumer activation and outcome instrumentation work.

## Refund and fairness principles

- Clear recurring/one-time label and cancellation route.
- Pro-rated or use-based refund policy stated before purchase.
- Refund credits for provider/product failure.
- Export access after cancellation for user-owned data.
- No countdown/scarcity unless objectively true.
