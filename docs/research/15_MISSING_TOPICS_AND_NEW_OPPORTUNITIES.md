## Strong new product opportunities

### 1. Career evidence vault

A candidate-owned structured record of achievements, skills, projects and confirmed metrics, with source and sensitivity flags. It powers documents, outreach and interviews without re-inventing facts.

### 2. Application packet

One job object containing alignment explanation, tailored resume version, letter, questions, outreach, notes, source/freshness, submission and outcome. This is the connective tissue competitors approach but do not make a visible trust moat.

### 3. Requirement evidence map

Instead of a score: `requirement → job quote → candidate evidence → status → action`. It supports correction and evaluation.

### 4. Job provenance card

Source, employer/ATS, published date, last verified, duplicate group, expiry and sponsorship evidence. Make data quality visible.

### 5. Search-status mode

Active, interviewing, paused, offer received, search ended. Changes dashboard, reminders and retention interpretation.

### 6. India hiring context layer

Notice period, CTC, serving-notice, location/relocation and joining constraints as first-class preferences, not generic profile fields.

### 7. Privacy-safe portfolio publisher

Original templates plus public-field preview and identifier blocking. Valuable for selected role families only, after immediate reference-page remediation.

### 8. Application quality clinic

A human-in-the-loop service/learning product for the first 100. It can reveal whether HireNudge's differentiator is software, expert service or a hybrid.
