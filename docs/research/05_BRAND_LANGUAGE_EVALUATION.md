# Brand-language evaluation

## Recommended language position

Working category hypothesis: **an India-first job-search operating system for higher-quality applications**. The safer immediate promise is narrower:

> Know which roles deserve your time. Build one truthful, tailored application. Keep every next step clear.

This is a positioning hypothesis, not an approved tagline.

## Language audit

| Current/public pattern | Risk | Recommended replacement |
|---|---|---|
| `Trusted by 10,000+ Job Seekers` | Count definition and source not supplied | Publish only with calculation, period and source; otherwise remove |
| `landing interviews faster` | Outcome/time causality not substantiated | `organize and improve your job-search workflow` until proven |
| `autopilot` / `bulk` | Conflicts with human review and anti-spam posture | `draft, review and send` / `assisted application` |
| `match score` without method | Can be read as hiring probability | `profile-to-role alignment` with requirements/evidence/gaps |
| `ATS score` | Suggests a universal employer system | Name exactly what is checked: parseability, required terms, format, evidence |
| `fix your resume` | Implies candidate deficiency | `make your evidence clearer for this role` |
| `complete your profile` via Gmail | Permission dark pattern | `Connect Gmail when you want HireNudge to send a reviewed email` |
| `perfect`, `guaranteed`, `job-winning` | Unverifiable outcome promise | `reviewed`, `role-specific`, `source-backed`, `editable` |

## Voice principles

- Specific before clever.
- Calm, respectful and non-judgmental.
- Explain why data is requested and how it changes a result.
- Separate fact, inference and suggestion in the UI.
- Never imply that AI knows an employer's private hiring decision.
- Use Indian hiring realities naturally—notice period, CTC, location—without stereotyping.
- For mobility, say `reported sponsorship` or `work-authorization requirement`, not `visa guaranteed`.

## Proof hierarchy

1. Product behavior the user can inspect.
2. Aggregate product telemetry with denominator and period.
3. Verified customer outcome with permission and methodology.
4. Customer quote with identity/permission and limited claim.
5. Company claim, clearly labeled.

Employer logos require a documented relationship or permission and must not imply employment outcomes HireNudge caused.

## Example interface copy

- Alignment: `Strong evidence for 6 of 9 stated requirements. Two requirements are missing from your profile; one is unclear.`
- AI edit: `Suggested from the job description. No supporting result was found in your profile—add evidence or discard.`
- Freshness: `Published 2 days ago · verified at source 4 hours ago · Greenhouse board.`
- Gmail: `HireNudge can request send-only access. It will not read your inbox in this version.`
- Voice: `We can describe structure, relevance, pace and filler-word counts. We do not score personality, honesty or employability.`

## Naming

`Nudge Studio` is stronger only if it unifies evidence-backed application artifacts. Avoid renaming as a cosmetic layer over disconnected resume, letter and outreach generators. The `Nudge` brand should mean a timely, explainable next action—not a constant notification.
