## Visual choice

One bar chart shows the planned mix of first-50 acquisition targets across five channels; it is a planning allocation, not observed performance. Charts were intentionally omitted for market sizing, market-gap ranking, competitor positioning, and product priorities because those inputs are assumption-driven ranges or categorical judgments rather than observed comparable measures. Tables preserve exact assumptions without implying false precision or a time-series trend that does not exist.
