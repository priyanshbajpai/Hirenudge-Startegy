# First 100 customers

## Objective

Acquire 100 **paying** active job seekers to validate the chosen wedge, workflow, willingness to pay and outcome measurement. This is a research cohort, not a vanity launch.

## Cohort design

Run four sequential cohorts of 25. Do not recruit the next cohort until the prior retrospective changes or confirms the product. Start with India → India; reserve a small, separately consented mobility research subgroup only if founder capacity exists.

Eligibility:

- India-based, English-speaking, actively switching in 30–60 days;
- 1–5 years of experience;
- target role within selected one or two role families;
- willing to use the workflow for at least two real applications;
- consents to aggregate product research; outcome reporting remains optional and clearly explained.

## Offer

A bounded `Application Quality Sprint`:

- profile/evidence setup;
- two real role-alignment reviews;
- two truthful, job-specific application packets;
- tracker/follow-up setup;
- one transcript-first interview practice when relevant;
- weekly group clinic and support.

Test price, do not assert it: e.g. three INR cells selected from interviews and unit-cost analysis. Display GST and refund/renewal terms clearly. Avoid a free-only cohort because it weakens willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Acquisition plan

| Source | Target | Method | Learning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Founder/team network and alumni | 25 | Direct, personalized invitation | Fast interviews and objections |
| Role-family communities | 25 | Educational clinic with paid follow-on | Message/channel fit |
| Two career creators/coaches | 25 | Disclosed affiliate/co-hosted clinic | Partner economics and trust |
| Referral from activated cohort users | 15 | Ask after verified value | Product-led sharing quality |
| Small paid retargeting only | 10 | Best organic proof concept | Earliest CAC signal, not scale |

Targets are planning allocations, not forecasts.

## Activation and outcome

Proposed activation within 24 hours:

1. profile/preferences completed enough for one role;
2. one live job saved and source verified;
3. alignment explanation reviewed;
4. at least one truthful application improvement accepted or rejected;
5. packet saved and next action scheduled.

Primary outcome candidate: **qualified interview responses per 10 high-fit, human-reviewed submitted applications**. Record the denominator, self-report nature and confidence interval. Supporting measures: activation time/rate, D1/D7/D30 use, application quality rubric, corrections to AI output, paid conversion/refund and support burden.

## Research cadence

- Day 0: expectation and baseline interview.
- Day 1: activation observation.
- Day 7: workflow/use interview and event review.
- Day 21/30: outcomes, value, churn and pricing interview.
- Weekly: founder memo with behavior, contradictions, risks and next decision.

## Stop/continue gates

Set numeric thresholds only after the first 10-user instrumentation calibration. At each cohort, continue only if:

- event data are reliable;
- users complete the core job-centered loop without concierge substitution;
- repeated use is tied to real applications;
- no material claims/privacy/job-data blocker remains;
- willingness to pay and contribution margin are directionally credible.

If users value only human resume review, treat that as a service/business-model signal rather than disguising it as SaaS activation.

## Operational roles

- Founder: ICP, offer, price and stop/continue decisions.
- Founder’s Office: recruitment, interviews, cohort operations and weekly memo.
- Product/engineering: event quality, bugs and bounded experiments.
- Trust/legal: consent, claims, outreach and data use.
- Support: response SLA and tagged failure modes.
