# HireNudge internal evidence intake

Owner: Priyansh Bajpai and Baskaran  
Founder: Divyansh  
Workspace identity: Founder’s Office

Purpose: replace assumptions in the HireNudge comparison and gap model with internally verified evidence. Do not place raw resumes, inbox content, immigration documents, phone numbers, or candidate-level personal data in the research Sheet.

## 1. Product and module inventory

Provide one row per live, beta, internal, planned, or retired module:

- module name and user problem;
- candidate or operator workflow;
- lifecycle state and release date;
- entitlement, credit consumption, dependencies and operating cost;
- integrations, extension, mobile and API status;
- event names used to measure activation and repeat use;
- product owner and technical constraint;
- source link to the live product, PRD, help article or release note.

## 2. Pricing and unit economics

Provide the current pricing and entitlement export with:

- currency, tax, billing period, trial, renewal and refund behavior;
- included and incremental credits;
- model, search, email, enrichment, support and human-service costs;
- realized discount, refund and failed-payment rates;
- plan-level signup, activation, conversion and churn counts.

## 3. Product analytics

Provide aggregate, de-identified funnel and cohort exports:

- visitor → signup → first role → first tailored application → reviewed application → submitted application;
- recruiter outreach created, reviewed and sent;
- interview, offer and accepted-offer events where users have explicitly supplied them;
- D1, D7, D30 and monthly active retention by signup cohort;
- conversion and qualified-interview rate by role, source, country target, plan and application method;
- event dictionary, identity rules, time zone and known tracking gaps.

The primary outcome candidate is qualified interviews per 10 high-fit, human-reviewed applications. Do not optimize for application volume before this is validated.

## 4. Customer evidence

Provide de-identified themes and links to the underlying evidence:

- support tickets and cancellation reasons;
- customer interview notes or recordings;
- onboarding drop-off reasons;
- resume, matching, outreach, interview and mobility failure modes;
- willingness-to-pay and purchase objections;
- evidence for any claimed interview, offer, placement or time-saved outcome.

## 5. Claims and proof

For every public claim, provide:

- exact wording and channel;
- approving owner;
- supporting sample, calculation and time period;
- whether it is a company claim, observed metric, testimonial or analyst inference;
- expiry or next-review date.

Unsubstantiated testimonials, employer logos and outcome claims remain excluded.

## 6. Trust, privacy and outreach

Provide current documentation for:

- privacy notice, consent, retention, deletion and data-export behavior;
- resume, inbox, recruiter-contact and mobility-data handling;
- model providers and subprocessors;
- extension permissions and security review;
- outreach limits, human review and opt-out behavior;
- incident response, access control and audit logging;
- accessibility, bias testing and candidate appeal.

TerraTern data must not be reused without explicit consent and a documented purpose.

## 7. Roadmap and constraints

Provide:

- committed work, capacity and release constraints for the next 90 days;
- dependencies on TerraTern, data providers, external APIs and hiring partners;
- founder objectives, revenue target, runway constraints and non-negotiables;
- experiments already attempted and their results.

## Completion labels

- `Internal verified`: supported by an internal export, system configuration or approved document.
- `Founder’s Office confirmed`: explicitly confirmed by Priyansh and Baskaran with a date.
- `Not supplied`: no internal evidence was provided; no gap score may be finalized from that field.
