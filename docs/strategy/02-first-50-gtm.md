# First 50 Paying Customers GTM

Working date: July 22, 2026
Goal: obtain willingness-to-pay, activation, repetition, and outcome evidence—not vanity registrations.

## Initial ICP hypothesis

English-first Indian job seekers with 0–5 years’ experience, actively applying within 30 days for private-sector roles in tech, product, analytics, operations, marketing, consulting, and adjacent functions.

First-50 composition:

- 30 employed professionals attempting a switch;
- 15 final-year students or recent graduates;
- 5 India-origin cross-border applicants as an exploratory cohort.

Keep cohorts separate in analysis; campus volume can hide lower willingness to pay.

## Offer

> Founding Job Search Sprint: 30 days of HireNudge, ten tailored application packs, tracker access, interview preparation, and one weekly group office hour.

Use an output guarantee, not an employment guarantee:

> If a customer cannot produce five usable, truthful application packs within 14 days after completing onboarding, refund the purchase.

Price ladder:

- customers 1–20: ₹499;
- customers 21–40: ₹999;
- customers 41–50: ₹1,499 only if activation and refunds remain healthy.

If payments are not available, collect a refundable deposit. Do not call free testers customers and do not sell lifetime access.

## Acquisition model

All conversions are planning assumptions until observed.

| Channel | Planned activity | Paid target | Why it is useful |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Founder/partner targeted outreach | 300 relevant prospects | 15 | Fast conversations and objection learning |
| Consented TerraTern segment | 400 relevant recipients | 12 | Tests the mobility adjacency without assuming it |
| Campus/alumni/bootcamp workshops | Three sessions with 50 attendees each | 10 | Efficient demos and observation |
| Helpful community teardowns | Ten evidence-led posts/sessions | 7 | Tests organic problem resonance |
| Customer referrals | Ask immediately after first value | 6 | Tests whether value is shareable |
| Total |  | 50 |  |

Use founder-led sales before paid ads. SEO compounds too slowly to be the main first-50 engine.

## Activation journey

Take the user to one real job instead of showing a generic dashboard tour:

1. Select target role, location, experience, salary band, and search deadline.
2. Upload resume or import profile information.
3. Paste one real job description.
4. Show match evidence and gaps.
5. Produce one editable, truthful tailored asset.
6. Save the role to the tracker.
7. Recommend the next two roles/actions and schedule a check-in.

Activation definition:

> Profile substantially completed + one real job description analysed + one tailored asset saved + three jobs tracked within 24 hours.

Aha moment:

> A usable application pack generated in under ten minutes without inventing experience.

North-star candidate:

> Weekly qualified applications completed with HireNudge.

Raw application volume is a guardrail risk, not a north star.

## Customer learning system

Every founding customer receives:

- baseline interview during onboarding;
- day-three behavioral check-in;
- day-seven outcome survey;
- day-14 or churn interview for a selected sample;
- explicit consent choice for product analytics and future research.

Complete at least 25 deeper interviews: 10 employed switchers, 10 students/recent graduates, and 5 paid users who failed to activate or requested a refund.

### Core interview questions

1. Walk through the last real application from discovery to submission.
2. Show the files, tabs, spreadsheets, messages, and tools used.
3. Where was the most time or confidence lost?
4. What has already been tried or purchased?
5. Which HireNudge output was actually used?
6. What was edited before submission, and why?
7. What almost caused abandonment?
8. What would replace HireNudge if it disappeared?
9. Who else has the same problem?
10. Will the user introduce that person now?

Learning ledger:

`Observation → segment → frequency → severity → workaround → willingness to pay → product implication → evidence → decision`

## Thirty-day cadence

### Days 1–3

- confirm ICP and exclusion criteria;
- install source attribution and funnel events;
- prepare consent, deletion, support, and claims processes;
- observe ten discovery users;
- launch the ₹499 cohort.

### Days 4–7

- get ten paid users and seven activated;
- fix only activation blockers;
- publish two evidence-led founder posts;
- run the first live application teardown.

### Week 2

- reach 25 paid and 17 activated;
- run two more workshops;
- compare students with employed switchers;
- test job-first versus resume-first onboarding and two primary messages.

### Week 3

- reach 40 paid and 27 activated;
- test the consented TerraTern segment;
- trigger referrals after first value;
- interview non-activators and refunds;
- publish anonymized learning from the first 25 users.

### Week 4

- reach 50 paid, 35 activated, and 25 repeat users;
- complete 25 deep interviews;
- calculate cash CAC and founder-time CAC;
- choose the validated ICP, price, promise, onboarding path, and six-week roadmap.

## Scorecard

Track every metric by persona and acquisition source:

- qualified lead-to-paid conversion;
- cash CAC and founder-time CAC;
- median time to first value, target under ten minutes;
- paid-to-activated within 24 hours, target 60–70%+;
- activated users completing a second tailored application, target 50%+;
- day-seven core-action retention, target 40%+;
- refund rate, target below 10%;
- assets actually submitted, not only generated;
- interviews per qualified submitted application versus baseline;
- referral rate;
- reasons for non-activation, churn, and refund;
- model/infrastructure cost per activated user.

Thirty-day proof is:

> 50 people paid, at least 35 reached value, at least 25 repeated the workflow, and one segment-channel-message combination is worth scaling.
