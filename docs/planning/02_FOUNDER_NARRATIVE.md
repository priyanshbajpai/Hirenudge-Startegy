# Founder narrative

Meeting length: 30–45 minutes  
Status: evidence-backed recommendation, not an approved company strategy

## The decision in one sentence

HireNudge should first prove that it helps a narrow India-based job-search cohort produce more truthful, better-qualified applications and manage the next step, while closing the claims, job-data, privacy and automation risks that currently weaken trust.

## 1. Where HireNudge is today

HireNudge publicly presents a broad suite spanning onboarding, resumes, optimisation, cover letters, job matching, outreach, tracking, interview preparation and autofill. The supplied screens show real product breadth. They do not establish live backend behavior, customer outcomes, retention or product-market fit.

The strongest current asset is coverage of the candidate journey. The largest strategic weakness is that the journey appears as a set of modules, cards and scores rather than one measurable workflow. Internal analytics, customer interviews, support evidence, unit costs, source contracts and roadmap constraints were not supplied. Public claims and visible features must therefore remain separate from verified performance.

## 2. Largest product gaps

Five foundations are missing or unverified:

1. a candidate-owned evidence profile;
2. a job-centered application packet;
3. explainable profile-to-role alignment;
4. job provenance, freshness and lifecycle quality;
5. a tracker that links reviewed applications to responses and interviews.

These foundations matter more than adding another standalone generator. Together they create the core loop: choose one live role, understand evidence and gaps, improve one truthful packet, submit with human review, then record the result.

## 3. Largest UX gaps

The observed dashboard makes many modules equally prominent. Gmail appears as a profile-completion step before outreach intent. Scores carry more visual weight than their evidence. Version history, uncertainty, source freshness, revocation and recovery states are not clear in the supplied screens.

The next UX task should be a prototype and usability test of the job-centered loop. A full visual redesign, mascot or motion-heavy landing page should follow evidence that the information architecture works.

## 4. What is already being improved

The team is discussing a modern light interface, connected modules, a journey-led dashboard, Nudge Studio, richer preferences, explainable matching, better job data, deeper interview practice, Gmail OAuth, contact discovery, lifecycle communication, competitor-informed creative and geographic corridors. Competitor-ad research is currently in progress.

None of these discussions is treated as approved or live. The planning register preserves this distinction.

## 5. New features worth considering

The strongest feature system is:

- a career evidence profile with field provenance;
- an application packet for each real job;
- a requirement evidence map instead of a score-first matcher;
- a job provenance card with source and last verification;
- immutable document versions linked to submissions;
- a search-status mode for active, interviewing, paused, offer and ended states;
- transcript-first, job-and-round interview practice;
- send-only, individually reviewed Gmail outreach, subject to launch gates.

The product advantage would come from connection and trust, not the number of generators.

## 6. What HireNudge should not build yet

Defer autonomous applications and outreach, inbox-reading personalisation, realtime voice before transcript value, global corridor coverage, employer-side screening, emotion or personality scoring, a large portfolio marketplace, scaled programmatic SEO, university customisation and a mascot-led maze or pinball redesign.

These items are either high-risk, weakly evidenced or downstream of core activation.

## 7. How the user journey should improve

The proposed first-value path is:

`Import truthful evidence → choose one source-verified role → review requirements/evidence/gaps → improve and save one packet → set the next action.`

Ask for notice period, CTC, authorization, Gmail and audio only when each field changes a current decision. The home should show search status, one primary next action, deadlines and recent outcomes. Nudge Studio should be an editing environment inside the application workflow, not a container for disconnected generators.

## 8. How brand language should improve

Working position:

> Know which roles deserve your time. Build one truthful, tailored application. Keep every next step clear.

This is a testable hypothesis, not an approved tagline. Replace `autopilot`, `bulk`, unqualified `match score`, universal `ATS score`, `perfect`, `guaranteed` and unsupported outcome language with specific, inspectable behavior. A Nudge should mean a timely, explainable next action.

## 9. Social media strategy

Use organic content as a learning engine. LinkedIn should carry founder-led application teardowns, product evidence and India hiring realities. Instagram should use concise reels and carousels around one problem and one proof object. X should carry research notes, myths and build decisions.

Every creative needs an original hook, visible proof, one CTA and claim review. Competitor ads supply format principles only. Paid acquisition should wait for stable activation, D7 repeat use, claim substantiation, end-to-end attribution and a contribution-margin CAC ceiling.

## 10. GTM strategy

Start with direct recruitment, alumni and operator networks, role-family communities, small paid application clinics and a small number of disclosed coach or creator partnerships. Use product behavior and paid participation to refine the offer. Treat free tools, referrals, university distribution and broad paid media as later channels with explicit gates.

## 11. Initial ICP

Provisional hypothesis: English-speaking, India-based employed professionals with roughly one to five years of experience who intend to switch private-sector roles within 30–60 days. Select one or two role families only after reviewing current users, job coverage and team access.

Start with India-to-India. Treat remote as a constraint, not a separate universal corridor. Test one India-to-country corridor later. Do not target every corridor at once.

## 12. First 100 customers

Resolve the existing first-50 versus first-100 contradiction. The recommended research design is four sequential cohorts of 25 paying users. The first 10 eligible users in cohort 1 form the calibration group and count toward the 100. Offer a bounded Application Quality Sprint with two real roles, two truthful packets, tracking and an interview-practice step when relevant.

Planning allocation, not forecast: founder/team network 25, role-family communities 25, two coaches or creators 25, activated-user referrals 15, and small paid retargeting 10. Review behavior, support burden, WTP and outcomes after every cohort before recruiting the next.

## 13. Largest risks and dependencies

The immediate risks are public sensitive-data exposure, unsupported claims, ambiguous automation, score misinterpretation, unknown job rights and freshness, Gmail scope/token security, contact-data compliance, AI-invented facts, TerraTern cross-use and missing internal evidence.

The critical chain is:

`Internal evidence → outcome and activation definition → job-centered prototype → calibration users → paid cohorts → retention and pricing evidence → acquisition scale.`

Claims/privacy remediation, job-source rights and Gmail/contact gates run in parallel.

## 14. Now, Next and Later

### Now

- close sensitive-data exposure;
- control claims and autonomy language;
- define activation and outcome metrics;
- audit job sources, freshness and duplicates;
- add alignment and truth-preservation rules;
- establish the minimum tracker/outcome loop;
- inspect Gmail scopes and security;
- establish TerraTern consent and core privacy/security controls.

### Next

- validate the job-centered architecture;
- build the evidence profile, application packet and version lineage;
- improve material controls;
- test transcript-first interview practice;
- add search status, lifecycle preferences and support operations;
- test pricing and the organic content engine;
- publish AI/provider transparency after controls are verified.

### Later

- voice practice;
- one mobility corridor;
- portfolio publishing for a proven role need;
- referrals, a single free tool and limited paid creative;
- university pilot;
- mascot and motion experiments.

## 15. Founder decisions

The next session should decide:

1. the outcome and activation definitions;
2. the provisional ICP, exclusions and role families;
3. the autonomy boundary;
4. the claims standard and remediation authority;
5. the application packet as the core object;
6. alignment terminology and numeric-score policy;
7. job-source rights and quality policy;
8. Gmail send-only and no-inbox boundary;
9. TerraTern controller, consent and cross-use rules;
10. the first-customer target, offer, price research and team capacity;
11. the sequencing of transcript and voice interview work;
12. the owner for analytics, job data, trust, claims and research cadence.

The dashboard should end with these decisions, not with a feature gallery.
