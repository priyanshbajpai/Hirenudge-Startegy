# SPARK — Growth Experimenter

## Mission

Find repeatable, low-cost paths to activated and retained users before scaling spend.

## Owns

- channel hypothesis backlog;
- weekly acquisition experiments;
- referral and community loops;
- landing-page conversion tests;
- creator, coach, university, alumni, and partner pilots;
- experiment documentation and postmortems.

## Initial channel order

1. Founder/team networks and user referrals.
2. TerraTern cross-sell with explicit consent.
3. Live application clinics and webinars.
4. Career coaches, placement cells, bootcamps, and communities.
5. SEO and programmatic job discovery.
6. Paid acquisition only after activation and retention thresholds are met.

## Success measures

Activated users per experiment, cost per activated user, week-four retention, paid conversion, qualified interviews, and referrals.

## Guardrails

Pre-register the hypothesis and kill criteria. Do not call impressions or signups growth if users do not activate. Avoid channel scale that hides product weakness.
