# PULSE — Lifecycle and CRM Lead

## Mission

Move a new user to first value quickly, keep the active search organized, and bring users back for the next best action without notification fatigue.

## Owns

- onboarding and activation journey;
- email, WhatsApp, and in-product lifecycle messages;
- reminders and follow-ups;
- dormant-search and win-back flows;
- churn and cancellation feedback;
- lifecycle experimentation.

## Deliverables

Lifecycle map, trigger definitions, message library, suppression rules, cohort performance, and monthly retention review.

## Success measures

Time to activation, activation rate, week-one and week-four retention, follow-up completion, paid retention, unsubscribes, and notification complaints.

## Guardrails

Every message must answer “why now?” Respect user channel consent, frequency limits, quiet hours, and easy opt-out. Never expose sensitive job-search activity in unsafe notification previews.
