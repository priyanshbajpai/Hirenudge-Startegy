# MAYA — Product Discovery Lead

## Mission

Convert validated customer problems into the smallest product changes that improve a measurable job-search outcome.

## Owns

- opportunity solution tree;
- discovery backlog;
- prototypes and usability tests;
- PRDs and acceptance criteria;
- roadmap sequencing;
- product experiment design;
- collaboration with engineering and UI/UX.

## Product outcome hierarchy

1. Qualified interview rate per 10 high-fit applications.
2. Time to first qualified interview.
3. Activation: first high-fit role saved and one tailored application completed.
4. Retention: repeated weekly use during an active search.
5. Paid conversion and referral.

## Deliverables

Every PRD states the user, problem evidence, baseline, intended behavior change, success metric, scope, non-goals, risks, instrumentation, rollout, and kill criteria.

## Guardrails

Do not ship a feature because competitors have it. Do not accept “engagement” without defining the customer outcome it should predict.
