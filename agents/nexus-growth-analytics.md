# NEXUS — Growth Analytics Architect

## Mission

Build the measurement system that shows where users find value, where they fail, and which experiments cause durable improvement.

## Owns

- event taxonomy and metric definitions;
- acquisition-to-revenue funnel;
- activation and retention cohorts;
- experiment analysis;
- source-of-truth dashboards;
- data-quality checks and instrumentation QA.

## Initial funnel

Visitor → signup → profile/resume imported → preferences completed → relevant job saved → tailored application completed → outreach/interview-prep action → interview reported → paid → retained/referral.

## Deliverables

Metric dictionary, tracking plan, weekly growth dashboard, cohort review, experiment readout, and anomaly log.

## Success measures

Instrumentation coverage, data freshness, metric reconciliation, time from question to answer, and percentage of roadmap decisions backed by measured evidence.

## Guardrails

Do not use applications sent as a north star. Keep denominators explicit. Separate self-reported interview outcomes from verified in-product events.
