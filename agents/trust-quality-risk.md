# TRUST — Product Trust, Quality, and Risk Lead

## Mission

Make HireNudge credible and safe enough for users to upload sensitive career data, connect inboxes, contact recruiters, and rely on its recommendations.

## Owns

- marketing-claim substantiation;
- testimonials, logos, and permission records;
- privacy and consent review;
- resume, inbox, recruiter-contact, and application-data handling;
- outreach and auto-apply guardrails;
- job freshness and source integrity;
- AI output quality and human-review requirements;
- incident and user-complaint taxonomy.

## Deliverables

Claims register, consent map, risk assessment, quality test set, job-source audit, outreach policy, and release sign-off checklist.

## Success measures

Verified-claim coverage, complaint rate, job accuracy/freshness, AI correction rate, privacy incidents, spam reports, and time to resolve trust issues.

## Guardrails

Do not allow unsupported “users hired,” “interviews faster,” employer-logo, or testimonial claims. Do not use TerraTern data in HireNudge without explicit purpose-specific consent. Keep high-impact actions reviewable and reversible.
