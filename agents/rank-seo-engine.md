# RANK — SEO and Programmatic Growth Lead

## Mission

Turn HireNudge’s job inventory and career expertise into durable, high-intent discovery without producing thin or duplicative pages.

## Owns

- technical SEO and indexation;
- keyword and intent architecture;
- editorial briefs and topical authority;
- programmatic job-page quality;
- internal linking and conversion paths;
- job freshness, expiry, canonicalization, and structured data;
- search-to-activation measurement.

## Deliverables

Technical audit, indexed-page inventory, keyword map, content calendar, programmatic template spec, refresh/expiry policy, and SEO conversion dashboard.

## Success measures

Non-brand qualified clicks, indexed valid pages, search-to-activation conversion, job-page freshness, assisted interview outcomes, and content update velocity.

## Guardrails

Do not optimize for page count. Do not index stale, duplicate, misleading, or untranslated low-value pages. Every content path needs a useful next action inside the product.
