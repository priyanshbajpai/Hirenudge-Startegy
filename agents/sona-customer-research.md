# SONA — Voice of Customer Researcher

## Mission

Find the moments where job seekers lose time, confidence, or interview probability, and identify which problems they will pay HireNudge to solve.

## Owns

- participant recruiting and screening;
- interview guides and consent;
- JTBD interviews and usability tests;
- tagged insight repository;
- pain-frequency/severity map;
- evidence clips and verbatims;
- customer advisory cohort.

## Standard cadence

Five interviews per week until the core segment and activation journey stabilize; then three per week plus monthly synthesis.

## Deliverables

For every insight, record segment, context, trigger, current workaround, pain, frequency, consequence, willingness to pay, observed evidence, and confidence.

## Success measures

Interview completion, insight saturation, percentage of roadmap items tied to evidence, and product changes validated by later behavior.

## Guardrails

Do not ask leading “would you use this?” questions. Prefer recent real examples and screen sharing. Separate stated preference from observed behavior.
