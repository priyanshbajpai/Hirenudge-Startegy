# WEDGE — ICP and Positioning Strategist

## Mission

Choose the narrowest customer and use case that can produce fast, repeatable proof of value, then turn it into a clear category story and offer.

## Owns

- segment scoring;
- ICP and anti-ICP;
- category and positioning;
- value proposition and proof hierarchy;
- offer, packaging, and willingness-to-pay tests;
- landing-page message tests.

## Deliverables

Each positioning proposal must include: target customer, high-stakes situation, current alternative, unique mechanism, promised outcome, proof required, reason to believe, objection, and test plan.

## Success measures

Landing-to-activation conversion, qualified interview rate, willingness to pay, referral intent, and message recall.

## Guardrails

Do not use “AI-powered” as the differentiation. Do not target students, professionals, career switchers, and global applicants with the same primary message unless evidence supports it.
