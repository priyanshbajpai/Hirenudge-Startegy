# HireNudge Founder’s Office Operating Context

This repository is the working strategy and execution system for HireNudge.

## Mission

Help Priyansh Bajpai and the Founder’s Office team build HireNudge from an early product into a trusted, measurable job-search business. Priyansh joins TerraTern on August 1, 2026 and is assigned to HireNudge as an early founding-team operator.

## Current strategic hypothesis

HireNudge should not compete as another generic AI resume tool. Its strongest potential wedge is an India-first global job-search operating system: help ambitious Indian job seekers move from role discovery to a high-quality application, recruiter outreach, interview readiness, and—where relevant—international mobility support through TerraTern.

This is a hypothesis, not a settled company decision. Validate it with product data, customer interviews, founder input, and willingness-to-pay tests before treating it as strategy.

## Working principles

- Start with the decision, customer, and measurable outcome.
- Separate verified facts, user-provided information, public claims, inference, and assumptions.
- Prefer observed behavior and product analytics over survey enthusiasm.
- Do not recommend scaling acquisition until activation and retention are understood.
- Treat interview rate per qualified application—not application volume—as the primary customer outcome candidate.
- Never invent customer traction, testimonials, product capabilities, or market numbers.
- Flag claims that need substantiation, especially user counts, hiring outcomes, employer logos, testimonials, and “auto-apply” performance.
- Protect job-seeker data. Resume, inbox, recruiter contact, application, and immigration data are sensitive.
- Obtain explicit consent before cross-using TerraTern leads or data for HireNudge.
- Keep outreach compliant and human-reviewed; avoid spam or fully autonomous high-volume application behavior.
- Build the smallest experiment that can change a decision.

## Primary users

The initial research wedge is English-speaking Indian professionals, students, and career switchers seeking better-fit jobs, with special attention to applicants targeting international roles. Do not assume this is the final ICP until tested.

When available, inspect:

- product analytics and event definitions;
- signup, activation, usage, retention, and conversion cohorts;
- current pricing and entitlement configuration;
- customer support and feedback;
- actual user interview recordings/notes;
- acquisition channel and SEO data;
- product roadmap and engineering constraints;

Public web research is useful for orientation and benchmarks, but it is not a substitute for company data.
